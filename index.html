    How the lean is scored. Each timeframe votes on three things: price above or below its moving average, RSI above 55 or below 45, and the direction of the RSI slope (half weight). D1 counts 3×, H4 2×, H1 1.5×, M15 1×. A score of +30 or more reads as leaning up, −30 or less as leaning down, anything between as mixed.

    The lean is chart structure only. The news panel lists scheduled releases from MT5’s economic calendar; it does not read headlines or judge what the market expects, so run your pre-session check before acting.
